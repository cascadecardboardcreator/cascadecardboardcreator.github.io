Privacy policy

Cascade Cardboard Creator collects no data at all. Here are the details.

Effective November 12, 2026

Cascade Cardboard Creator is a building and recycling game for children. It is built to work entirely on your device. We don't collect, store or share any personal information, from children or anyone else, because none of it ever reaches us.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts or sign-ins, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party tracking or data-collecting SDKs. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Progress and settings

Your child's progress (the builds they have finished and how they painted them, their own Free Make inventions, what is on the Maker Room shelf, tidy stars and wishes) and the voice and music switches are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They never leave it.

Voices

The characters' lines are read aloud by your device's built-in speech voice. Cascade Cardboard Creator hands the words to that voice on the device and doesn't record or send anything. The app never listens: it doesn't use the microphone.

Purchases

The app has no ads and nothing for sale inside it. If that ever changes, the App Store or Google Play would handle any payment, and this page would be updated first.

Permissions

None. Cascade Cardboard Creator doesn't use the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or internet.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to review, share or delete. This is true for children under 13 (COPPA), and in every country.

Deleting your data

Deleting Cascade Cardboard Creator from your device removes all of its data.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
